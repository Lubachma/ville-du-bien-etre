# 🏙️ Ville du bien-être

**Grow a city by taking care of yourself.** Real-life activities with a timer earn coins, and coins build a living isometric town: it follows the time of day and the seasons, its residents stroll around and visitors drop by.

**[▶ Try it live](https://lubachma.github.io/ville-du-bien-etre/)** — nothing to install, no account. In English or French.

100 % local — your city stays on your device (browser storage). Installable on iPhone and Android, works offline. Made to be shared with friends: everyone keeps their own city, and visits the others' with a simple link.

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| ⏱️ **Focus** | Keep the app on screen while the timer runs (5, 10 or 20 s of grace to read a notification). Leaving longer interrupts the session: the time already done stays in the journal, without coins |
| 🚶 **Free activity** | Walk, sport, music: lock the phone or switch apps, the timer keeps going and the result is recorded when you come back — even after closing the app |
| 🎲 **Random draw** | Can't decide? Draw from your wishes (+20 %) or your chores (+50 %), filtered by available time, energy and place. One-off and recurring tasks leave the draw once done and come back on schedule |
| 🔁 **Routines** | Chain 2 to 6 activities with a free break between steps; progress survives a reload |
| 🎯 **Weekly goal** | A flexible weekly target (minutes and active days, all domains or one), plus a building to aim for; the week starts on Monday or Sunday |
| 🏗️ **City** | 51 items: 30 buildings unlocked by the minutes of their domain, the Clock Tower, 17 decorations and 3 wonders; land up to 11×11. A shop organised by section, with shortcuts and silhouettes of what comes next. Buildings level up with practice, windows light up at night. Name your city and export it as a PNG |
| 🌳 **Wonders** | Great Tree, Lighthouse and Big Wheel: one of each, built in four stages unlocked by total minutes (from 1,500 to 7,600 min). The Great Tree blossoms in the colours of the domains practised this week |
| 🧩 **Neighbourhood sets** | 12 trios to discover by placing three items within the same 3×3 square (a bench and a tree near the library, for instance); a shared paving ties them together |
| 🔨 **Lively building** | Scaffolding, dust and a small bounce on every placement; a note that rises as you place things in a row, confetti when a wonder is inaugurated. No animation when the phone asks for reduced motion |
| 🌤️ **Living town** | The town follows its own time zone: dawn, daytime, golden hour, dusk and a starry night. Four seasons (hemisphere guessed or chosen), a weather of the day (sun, clouds, rain, snow, fog, a rainbow after the rain), chimney smoke and summer fireflies. Residents stroll around, and one of them does the same activity as you today. Tap the town to see it full screen, with zoom (up to × 3) and panning. Nothing decays when you stay away |
| 🦔 **Visitors** | 12 visitors drop by about 5 days out of 7, depending on the season, the weather and what your town offers them (a bench, a pond, a bandstand…). Tap them to meet them and receive a small gift (2 M); the notebook keeps the ones you met and gives a hint for the others |
| 🎉 **Festivals** | Six festivals a year, including your town's birthday: bunting, a decor for each festival and, on the first visit, a gift (5 M, 10 M for the birthday). Nothing is time-limited: festivals come back every year |
| 🗓️ **Weekly challenges** | Every week, three small challenges sized to you (practice, moments, discovery), based on your past weeks: 5 M each, and one challenge you can swap once a week. Nothing is lost if you don't make it |
| 📬 **Weekly review** | At the start of the week, “Your week”: practice time, active days, domains, highlights, the goal and the progress on the week before, always put positively; then the challenges of the week ahead. The card stays at the top of the Journal |
| 🙂 **Mood** | After a session, “How do you feel?” in one tap (five faces, or “Skip”). The Journal shows what makes you feel good, domain by domain, and a gentle card when the last sessions have been hard. Moods stay on the device, never in a link or a picture |
| 💌 **A friend's town** | “Share my town” sends a link that carries the town, with no server. Your friend visits it full screen, under their own sky, and keeps its postcard in the notebook; a received link can also be pasted into the app |
| 📅 **Recaps** | Each new month, “October 2026”: figures, domains, highlights and what happened in the town; in January, the whole year. A 1080 × 1350 picture to share, without moods |
| 💭 **Residents' wishes** | Every day, a building would love a decoration right next to it (“I'd love a bench right next to me.”): a bubble above it, a card under the town with “Place a bench”. Granting it brings 3 M and a thank-you. A wish never expires, and “Not now” puts it away without another word |
| ⭐ **Harmony** | Five stars to earn by arranging your town: greenery, variety, little touches, neighbourhoods and evening lights, each with a gentle hint. A star you earn stays yours; all five give the Flower arch |
| 🎧 **Ambient sounds** | During a session, if you like: rain, waves, campfire, wind or soft noise, made by the browser (no files, works offline). Off by default, they fade out before the end sound |
| 🐾 **A companion** | After a first session, a rabbit, a marmot or a bear cub that you name settles in your town: it strolls by day, sleeps at night, and does every session with you (reading, stretching, meditating, watering or painting). It has a kind word every day and never asks for anything. Time spent together grows your friendship over ten levels that give nine accessories; the last one gives the Hut |
| 🌬️ **Breathe together** | In Calm sessions, if you like: 5 s to breathe in, 5 s to breathe out, with the companion gently growing as you breathe in; nothing moves with reduced motion |
| 📒 **Journal** | Today, streak, reviews and recaps, weekly goal and challenges, 7-day chart, progress per domain, what makes you feel good, latest sessions |
| 🏆 **Collection and achievements** | A notebook in the Journal: 48 achievements (first steps, domains, times of day, regularity, time, city, living town, rituals, harmony, companion), 7 of which give a decoration; the collection of all 52 items, the sets discovered, the visitors met, the festivals celebrated and the postcards of friends' towns. An existing city receives the achievements it already earned in one go |
| 🌱 **Kind streak** | 0 to 2 rest days per week, a pause mode, a +20 % welcome-back gift after a break, and never a guilt message. The streak can be hidden; its bonus still applies |
| 🌍 **Languages** | English and French, following the phone or chosen in the settings. Switching is instant, without a reload; example activities you haven't renamed follow the language |
| 💾 **Backups** | Export to a file (on iPhone: “Save to Files”) or a text, restore on another device; a reminder appears after 14 days |

### Rewards

- 💰 **1 M coins** per completed minute
- 🔥 **Streak**: +10 % per active day, up to +50 %; rest days (2 per week by default) and pauses keep it going
- 🌅 **Welcome back**: +20 % on the first session that starts a new streak, after at least 2 days without one
- 🎲 **Draw**: first draw accepted +20 % (wish) or +50 % (chore) from 3 available ideas, halved on each paid re-roll
- 🗓️ **Challenges**: 5 M per challenge completed, up to 15 M a week
- 💭 **Wishes**: 3 M per wish granted, at most one a day; ⭐ **harmony stars**: 10 M each, once
- 🧭 **5 domains** — Mind, Body, Calm, Home, Creativity (Esprit, Corps, Calme, Foyer, Créativité in French) — each unlocking its own buildings, then the Clock Tower after 1,000 minutes in total and the wonders from 1,500

## ⚙️ Settings

- **Language**: automatic (the phone's language, otherwise English), Français or English.
- **Sessions**: grace period (5, 10 or 20 s), keep the screen on, end sound (chime, bowl or bells; soft, normal or loud), ambience during sessions and its volume (on iPhone, it stops when the screen locks), guided breathing in Calm sessions, vibration.
- **Streak**: rest days per week, pause, hide the streak.
- **Week and reminder**: the week starts on Monday or Sunday; a daily reminder added to your calendar (`.ics` file or Google Calendar) — the app itself sends no notifications.
- **Rituals**: reviews at the start of the week and month (turned off, they no longer open by themselves and stay in the Journal), weekly challenges, asking how you feel after a session, residents' wishes.
- **Companion**: show it or not (in the town and during sessions), change its animal or name; without one, welcome it later.
- **Appearance**: theme (auto, day, night), **town sky** (time of day or match appearance), **seasons** (automatic, northern or southern hemisphere) and **living town** (turning it off stops continuous motion to save battery).
- **Strict mode** (Guided Access or app pinning), **backup** and **start over**.

## 📱 Install on a phone

1. Open **<https://lubachma.github.io/ville-du-bien-etre/>** (the HTTPS address, not an HTML file received as an attachment).
2. **iPhone**: in Safari, **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**. **Android**: Chrome menu → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Launch it from its icon. In the settings, wait for **“Available offline”** before relying on offline mode.

Install it before you start: a city started in a browser tab doesn't move to the installed app by itself (transfer it with **Settings → Backup**). The in-app help lives in **Settings → Getting started and installing the app**.

## 🔒 Data & privacy

- Progress stays in the browser's local storage. No account, no analytics, no ads; the host only receives the requests needed to download the site, never your activities.
- **A town link** only carries your town's name, the size of the land, its items (type, place, wonder stage, level), the Great Tree's flowers and a random identifier — never your minutes, your journal or your moods. It travels in the address fragment (`#ville=…`), which browsers don't send to the host.
- **Settings → Backup** exports a file or a text and restores it on another device. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app can delete the city — keep a copy.
- **VBE1** backups (first version) remain importable; **VBE2** exports also carry modes, routines and goals. A running session isn't exported.
- Progress from the first version is copied to a separate v2 storage without deleting the original, and unreadable data is preserved before any repair.
- **A stale tab** (left on an older version because the update was accepted in another tab) can no longer erase what it doesn't know: on every write, it puts back, as they were, what a newer version had saved (unknown keys at every level, unknown entries of tables such as achievements or gifts, unknown items of lists with ids such as the town's tiles or the journal). A restore or “Start over” replaces everything and takes nothing back from the old city, and another tab still open on it can't bring it back.
- **A stale tab reloads by itself** as soon as it is idle: no session is running in it, no “Undo” is still offered after a deletion and, while it is on screen, it has no open window, full-screen view, placement or text being typed; in the background, only a session, its end screen and typed text (on the page or in an open window) still hold it back. The **“Update”** banner stays up until then.
- **The town clock** keeps the day from going back (weather, visitor, festivals, date of sessions) and now moves forward only when a session ends — completed, interrupted or abandoned. The sky (light, time of day, Clock Tower) always follows the phone's real time, so a date set forward by mistake and then corrected no longer freezes the town. A session done while the date was set forward is the exception: it keeps its day, and the next ones attach to it until the real date catches up. A visitor or wish gift is never received for a day earlier than the latest gift of the same kind.
- An **impossible date** in the data (an edited backup, for instance) no longer blocks the app, and a city that was already blocked repairs itself when the app opens. A backup pasted as text is accepted even if it is broken into lines or has spaces in it.
- **Rule for future stages**, because the protection above relies on it: never change the meaning of an existing field — add a new field instead; and don't keep data that matters in a list without ids (pauses, routine steps), where the current version always wins.
- **Known limits**: unknown tiles from a newer version are put back at the end of the list (if the stale tab placed a tile on the same square, its own tile wins); unknown journal entries also go at the end, even in a full journal (150 sessions), where the version that knows them doesn't read them back.

## 🚀 Quick start

```bash
npm ci
npm run build      # src/ → app.js + sw.js
npm run serve      # http://127.0.0.1:8766
```

Requires Node.js ≥ 20 for the tooling only — the published site is plain static files.

## 🛠️ Stack

Vanilla JavaScript, no framework and no runtime dependency · Canvas 2D isometric renderer · Service Worker (offline first, updates on demand) · Web Locks (one session across tabs) · Screen Wake Lock · Web Audio (end sounds) · Web Share (backup and calendar files) · Intl (plurals, dates and numbers per language) · node:test + Playwright

The build is a small Node script: it concatenates `src/` into `app.js` and stamps the service worker with a content hash.

| Command | Role |

| `npm run board` | Drawing board in `test-results/planche/` (with `PLAYWRIGHT_CHANNEL=chrome` where Playwright's own Chromium isn't installed): city by day and by night, wonders by stage, a 320 px screen, notebook thumbnails, times of day, seasons, weather, visitors and festivals, rituals (review, recap and its picture, visit, Journal), wish of the day, harmony, the Flower arch and a session screen with its ambience, the companion (poses, accessories, session, breathing, end screen, town by day and by night with its Hut); drawing time of one frame with a 4× slower processor |

Browser tests need `npx playwright install chromium`, or `PLAYWRIGHT_CHANNEL=chrome` to reuse an installed Chrome; `BROWSER=webkit` runs them on WebKit, Safari's engine, after `npx playwright install webkit`.

**Never edit `app.js` or `sw.js` by hand** — they are generated by the build.

## 🚢 Deployment

GitHub Pages, through the **manual** workflow [`pages.yml`](.github/workflows/pages.yml): **Actions → Déployer sur GitHub Pages → Run workflow**, or `gh workflow run pages.yml`. It builds, checks, runs the unit and browser tests, then publishes only the static files of `dist/` — a push alone never publishes. All paths are relative, so `dist/` also works at the root of any static host.

On every push and pull request, the **Vérifier** workflow ([`verify.yml`](.github/workflows/verify.yml)) builds the app, verifies that `app.js` and `sw.js` are up to date in the repository, runs `npm run check` and the unit tests, then the browser tests on Chromium and on WebKit (Safari's engine), each in its own job. It publishes nothing: publishing stays manual, through `pages.yml`.

Installed apps get the new version with an **“Update”** prompt, applied after the running session; the city is kept. Before sharing a version, check on a real iPhone and Android: install from the link, first offline launch, free activity with the screen locked, coming back after closing, backup/restore and update.
